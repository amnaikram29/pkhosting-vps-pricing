FAQs

Setup, billing & support

Quick answers on billing, plan changes, currency, refunds and root access.

How does annual billing work?

You pay for 10 months and get 12. The per-month price on the card is the yearly total divided by 12.

Can I change plans later?

Moving up is allowed any time, and the unused part of the current period is credited. This is a demo, so there is no real account.

Which currency am I charged in?

Prices are set in rupees. USD and GBP are shown for reference at fixed rates, not live rates.

Is there a refund window?

Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next renewal. Annual plans have a 7-day refund window.

Do I get root access?

Yes, on every plan, with your choice of Linux image.
